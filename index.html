Who was George Washington?

George Washington was the first president of the United States of America and helped to lead us to independence. Given the well known history of Washington leading our country in the fight against the British, it may be surprising for many to learn that he started out fighting for, and not against the British. After the revolution ended and Washington became president, the nation was trying to define what it was and was not, and this led to a widely unknown fact that there were some people who wanted to crown him king. There are many examples of people nowadays not knowing certain facts or even most of what happened during the life of Washington. The purpose of this website will be to go over some key parts of Washington's life.
